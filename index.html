Mathematics

Numbers, algebra, geometry and data handling.

View Topics

1. Rational Numbers

Rational numbers are numbers that can be written in the form p/q, where q is not zero.

Examples: 1/2, -3/4, 5, 0.25

2. Linear Equations

A linear equation contains a variable with power 1.

Example: x + 5 = 12

Therefore, x = 7.

3. Squares and Square Roots

The square of a number is obtained by multiplying it by itself.

Example: 12² = 144

4. Cubes and Cube Roots

The cube of a number is the number multiplied by itself three times.

Example: 5³ = 125

5. Comparing Quantities

Important concepts include percentage, profit, loss, discount and simple interest.

6. Algebraic Expressions

Algebraic expressions contain numbers, variables and mathematical operations.

7. Geometry

Important topics include quadrilaterals, polygons, angles and practical geometry.

Science

Physics, Chemistry and Biology fundamentals.

View Topics

1. Crop Production

Agricultural practices include preparation of soil, sowing, irrigation, harvesting and storage.

2. Microorganisms

Microorganisms are very small living organisms. Some are useful while others can cause diseases.

3. Force and Pressure

Force is a push or pull. Pressure is force acting on a unit area.

4. Friction

Friction is a force that opposes motion between two surfaces in contact.

5. Sound

Sound is produced by vibrating objects and travels through a medium.

6. Light

Light travels in straight lines and can be reflected from surfaces.

7. Human Reproduction

Reproduction is the biological process through which living organisms produce new individuals.
